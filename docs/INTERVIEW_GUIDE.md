# Interview Guide

Be ready to explain:

1. Why vector search is insufficient for changing facts.
2. Why provenance and temporal validity matter.
3. Why LLM output is a proposal rather than proof.
4. Why deterministic verification is stronger for executable tasks.
5. Why tool authorization must not depend on model obedience.
6. How prompt injection differs from memory poisoning.
7. Why Best-of-N is not automatically MCTS.
8. How you allocate compute under a budget.
9. Why local/cloud routing exists.
10. How you measured whether the architecture improved anything.
11. What failure modes remain.
12. How you would scale the executor and graph layer.
